Reset: a pinhole straight onto the plunger

A 1.2 mm hole through the right wall lines up with plunger. There’s no mechanism to wear out.

pin outside

The hole is countersunk on the outside so a paperclip finds it. On the inside, a trough in the lid carries the pin the last few millimetres to the plunger, so it can’t slip off beside the switch. The switch clicks after 0.25 mm and bottoms at 0.45.
