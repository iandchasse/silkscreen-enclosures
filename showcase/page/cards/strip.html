Light strip

A flex circuit puts six warm/cool pairs of side-view LEDs along the panel’s left edge and plugs into the board’s frontlight connector, J3. The light grazes the page from under the bezel. One strip fits both readers; each case size has a light-strip build that makes room for it.

    What sticks where

    The strip arrives with its own tape: JLC laminates 3M 9077 onto the back of the two front stiffener pieces. You add a few pieces of Kapton in the reader. Two places must stay free of tape and stiffener, because that is where the flex bends.

    What the page sees

    Grazing light falls off steeply: the model puts the page 2 mm from the lit edge at about 230 times the far edge on the 4.26″, and the pairs smooth out along the edge within about 10 mm. A real e-paper film scatters, so the far side looks brighter than this geometry predicts.
