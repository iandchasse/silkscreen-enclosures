The case, built around the board

One printed case in two sizes, 4.26″ and 3.68″, each as a standard build and a light-strip build; the 4.26″ also comes chinless, without the bottom keys, with either panel. Pick a size and a build above the model: the 3D view is the CadQuery model’s own mesh, every drawing in the tour is a section cut through that mesh, and the downloads are its print files.

AI-assisted work, not the official Silkscreen enclosure. These cases are a baseline printable enclosure for the early days of the board, designed with substantial AI assistance; community efforts toward a human-built case are ongoing. See Silkscreen’s AI disclosure. The light-strip builds are in development and not released.
